I spend a lot of time exploring new AI tools — testing LLM inference setups, RAG pipelines, and agentic workflows. Until now, most of those notes lived on my machine. This blog is where I'll publish them.

## What I'll write about

- **AI news** — noteworthy model releases, tooling updates, and ecosystem changes, with a practitioner's take.
- **Technical newsletters** — hands-on notes from my own experiments: what I tried, what worked, and what didn't.

## How this blog works

Posts are written in Markdown and the site is a fully static React export hosted on GitHub Pages, so pages load fast and every post gets its own shareable URL with an RSS feed at [/rss.xml](/rss.xml).

If you work with Generative AI, RAG, or LLM serving, I hope something here saves you an afternoon of debugging. Let's get into it.
