I spend a lot of time exploring new tools and ideas — testing LLM inference setups, RAG pipelines, agentic workflows, and plenty outside AI too. Until now, most of those notes lived on my machine. This is where I'll publish them.

## What I'll write about

Whatever earns it. Some pieces will be technical deep-dives, others will be
short notes, opinions, or things I wish someone had written down for me.
No fixed beat, no newsletter schedule — just articles worth reading.

## How this site works

Articles are written in Markdown and the site is a fully static React export hosted on GitHub Pages, so pages load fast and every article gets its own shareable URL with an RSS feed at [/rss.xml](/rss.xml).

If something here saves you an afternoon of debugging — or makes you think — then it did its job. Let's get into it.
